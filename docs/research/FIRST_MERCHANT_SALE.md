# First merchant sale: usability sessions

Status: protocol prepared; no merchant sessions have been conducted. Recruit 3–5 real merchants who have not used the new setup guide. Use their own products and a test store. Payments and payouts remain pending; do not collect card details or attempt a live charge.

Allow 30–40 minutes per merchant, preferably on the device they normally use for work. Ask permission before recording. Explain: “We are testing the product, not you. Please say what you expect to happen.” Do not coach unless they cannot continue; record each intervention.

Ask the merchant to:

1. Add two products with prices, images and stock; include a variant if they sell configurable products.
2. Apply their branding and explain what they expect YAPI to preserve.
3. Set up pickup and/or delivery, including location and service area.
4. Find payment readiness and explain whether real money can be collected today.
5. Run the automatic check. For a simple product, personally add an item, open Pedido, change quantity and open the test payment form. Along the way, try the store's enabled pickup/delivery options; for delivery, try a blank and then completed address. Digital-only orders skip physical fulfillment. For configurable products, test the platform's variant/extra selection separately: the automatic check verifies its handoff but does not complete option selection or payment simulation.
6. Find the publish action and explain which version customers will see. In the research session use a disposable test store; do not publish a real merchant's unfinished store.
7. Find the growth report and explain what “Pago real confirmado” and the uncompleted steps mean.

Record for each task:

| Participant | Task | Completed without help? | Time | Exact confusing wording/action | Help required | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

A blocker prevents completion; a major issue causes a wrong order or incorrect belief about payment readiness; a minor issue causes recoverable hesitation. Preserve quotes as quotes and observations separately from interpretations. Avoid recording customers' addresses, credentials or financial details.

After all sessions, prioritize repeated blockers and incorrect payment expectations. Assign each finding an owner and a reproducible acceptance criterion. Repeat the failed task with the affected merchant after fixing it. Success means a merchant can complete the setup and test purchase without coaching and correctly understands that live payments are still pending.
