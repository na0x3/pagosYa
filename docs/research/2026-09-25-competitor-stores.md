# Competitor stores — Clyro, Amboras, Webild (2026-09-25)

Owner's ask: "go study stores generated by amboras, clyro and other website generators such as webild and i want my
stores to look as good as those, no matter what product the vendor is selling". Captures: headless Chromium at
1440 and 390 of peakclyro.myshopify.com (the only Clyro showcase store still online; Pawland is paused, the rest
are gone), amboras.com and amboras.com/examples (21 stores), webild.io/templates. Earlier: the owner's screenshot
of Clyro's "Choose a template" (Aura / cosmetics, Axiom / supplements, Mogged / men's grooming) and
`webild-browser-motion-analysis-2026-09-20.md`.

## What each one does

| | How a store starts | What the result looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Clyro** | Pick a template (Aura, Axiom, Mogged, Peak…), each a complete Shopify theme for a vertical; then "customize with AI". | Peak (cereal): one loud brand world end to end — Luckiest Guy / Archivo Black display, thick black borders and offset shadows, wavy section edges, a marquee band, per-product colored cards, a lifestyle photo strip, a giant statement with the product cut out over the type, sticker badges. About 9 sections, 4,400px on desktop. |
| **Amboras** | "Tell the AI what you sell" → full store; AI product-image enhancing ("sharper details, clean backgrounds, on-brand AI styling"). | 21 showcase stores are nearly all **one hero product**: big gallery on the left, a very complete buy box on the right. Classic DTC. |
| **Webild** | Pick a template by vertical (coffee shop, agency, real estate, auto detailing, HVAC…) or prompt. | Agency-style marketing pages: premium photography, marquees, 3D, video scroll. |

## The Amboras buy box (every example has it)

Announcement bar with 3–4 claims · logo lockup with a small tagline · star rating + review count · a short eyebrow
of claims · large display product name · one-line subtitle · attribute chips · price with unit context ("7.5 lb
bag · about 30 meals") · visual option pickers (icons, swatches, sizes) · purchase choices (one-time vs subscribe
with the saving shown) · a full-width contrasting button that says the price ("Start for $33.60 — first bag") ·
a row of 3 trust icons · 4 thumbnails. Background colour pulled from the product/packaging.

## What they share, in order of impact

1. **The photos are professional.** Every example has studio-grade product photography on a background chosen for
   the product, plus lifestyle shots. This is the single largest visible gap for a merchant who uploads phone
   photos. Amboras sells it as a feature (AI image enhancing).
2. **One committed brand world per store.** Display face, palette, border/shadow language and a motif carried into
   every section (Peak: black ink borders + wavy edges + stickers). Not "a nice default with the store's colour".
3. **Starting points are finished stores, made by designers.** Clyro and Webild both lead with a template gallery;
   the AI customizes a strong base instead of inventing a layout from nothing.
4. **Conversion-dense product page.** The buy box above does most of the selling.
5. **Ornament that belongs to the product:** marquee bands, stickers, wavy/torn section edges, product cut-outs
   overlapping headlines.

## What this means for pagosYa

pagosYa has spent many rounds improving the generation prompt and adding kit variety (see
`docs/STOREFRONT_DESIGN_LEVEL_UP.md`, `docs/STOREFRONT_VISUAL_DIVERSITY_PLAN.md`, the unique-stores program).
The competitors' quality does not come from a better prompt. It comes from (1) designer-made complete templates as the starting
point and (2) fixing the merchant's photos. Plan, in order:

1. **Template gallery ("Plantillas")** — many complete, modern stores across verticals, each designed to the bar
   above, with neutral placeholder photos; the merchant picks one, it fills with their products, and they swap
   photos and text. (Store-themes T3 + T5, scaled up.)
2. **Photo studio** — gpt-image-2 (already wired in `apps/api/src/visual-studio/visual-ai.service.ts`) turns a
   merchant's product photo into studio shots and mockups on a background matched to the template.
3. **"Producto único" store type** — one product, the Amboras-style buy box and a story built around it.
4. **YAPI starts from a template** chosen by what the merchant sells, then applies the identity engine (T4), instead
   of generating from scratch.
