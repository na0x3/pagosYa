# Template polish programme (2026-10-28)

Owner, 2026-10-28: «lets add more templates but polish them well, very well polish them, so each run is perfect»;
on avril: «too short… the store longer with more pictures… more sections». Order chosen: fix the existing templates
first, then four new ones (grocery/market, beauty/skincare, home & decor, kids/toys/gifts).

## The bar every template meets on every build

1. **Long:** a home of at least ~8,000 px on desktop with 9+ blocks (hero, products, 4–6 business sections, story,
   values, reviews/trust, closing, footer). No store ends after the catalog and two text boxes.
2. **Photo-led:** every business section carries a photo when the store has one left; photos never repeat on the
   home (no `index % length` cycling); a section past the last photo is laid out for words, not left with a hole.
3. **The merchant's material first:** brand pictures and the video sent with the first message are used before
   product photos; up to 12 photos reach the fill (today 8).
4. **Works:** search (header field, magnifier, Enter) opens buscar.html with results; the results page is styled
   with photos; no dead buttons, no overlapping header items, no text over a busy photo.
5. **Calm rules kept:** one accent, normal-size cards, no rainbow bands, no dark text-only bands, no photo walls of 3+
   unless asked, no floating shapes/petals unless asked.

## Phase 1 — existing templates (esencial + its families, catalogo, maison/Atelier, mesa, ritual, tecno)

- Section photos with no repeats in every skeleton (done for maison, mesa, ritual, tecno on 2026-10-28; catalogo and
  esencial still to do).

- Styled search results.
- An automatic check after every build (page height, block count, photos shown, repeats, dead search) whose findings
  are logged and fixed before a template counts as done.
- Live proof: avril, ZARA, PEPPA please, DooBoo, Remolino rebuilt on the main line; screenshots compared.
