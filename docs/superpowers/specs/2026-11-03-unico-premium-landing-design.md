# Único premium landing — spec (2026-11-03)

Owner, after the lively work: «stores we generated by now for one-product stores… they don't look great; I would like them to look
like that» — references https://nubecorp.pages.dev and Shopify's Expanse theme (Urban Living preset). Choices: **mix both** —
nubecorp's structure and motion with Expanse's premium finish; approach **A — rebuild Único's home from library sections plus an
Expanse finish on its theme** (YAPI fills, nothing free-drawn). Also: «this hero I don't like» (Crocante/Oh Belly `cover-full`
look `right`: mixed alignment, words over the photo's subject) → **words in a solid panel**.

Builds on the lively spec (2026-11-03-lively-one-product-stores-design.md): buy bar, pills, hotspots, chip band, bubble ping,
button feel and the colourful/calm mood stay as built. Queued separately (own specs later): growing stores (Único → catalogue
template when products are added), the pagosYa browser button, AliExpress's official API.

## 1. The page, top to bottom (Único's `layout`)
1. **Announcement bar** — Esencial's `announce` variant with a real fact (delivery city, or the countdown when a date was given).
2. **Split hero** — new library section `hero-split`: left the best lifestyle photo (4:5, rounded, pills float on it); right a panel:
   eyebrow with a pinging dot (city or category), the headline (display font), one or two lines, two buttons — «Ver el producto»
   (scrolls to the buy box) and «Ver colores» (scrolls to the colour cards; only when they show). On phones the photo is on top.
   Words never sit over a photo; one alignment (left) for the whole panel.
3. **Word band** — `strip-marquee` (5–6 short product words) above a `wave-divider`.
4. **Colour cards** — new section `variant-cards`, drawn by the kit from the product's variants: one card per colour value that has
   its own photo (4:5), the colour name and «desde Bs …» (hidden while the price is still to confirm). Tapping a card selects that
   colour in the buy box and scrolls there. Shown only with ≥ 2 colours that have photos.
5. **Buy box** — `product-hero` (the kit's product block). The pills move to the hero photo; the hero no longer repeats them here.
6. **Benefits band** — `benefits-orbit` on a light accent tint: 4 icon benefits around one photo.
7. **Hotspots** — `detail-hotspots` (only with a detail photo).
8. **Chip band** — `chips-marquee` (real reviews when the store has published ones, else product facts).
9. **Comparison** — `compare-table` «Este producto vs lo de siempre», only from facts the listing or merchant gave.
10. **FAQ** — `faq-split` with a new calm look `lines` (thin dividers, plus/minus, no grey pills).
11. **Closing** — `closing-photo` (21:9 photo, one line, «Comprar» button).

## 2. Expanse finish (Único theme only)
- **Header** — new Esencial header variant `shop`: logo left; a short menu (Producto · Colores · Preguntas — anchors on the home,
  the store's pages elsewhere); search, account, cart and a solid «Comprar» button (scrolls to the buy box on the home, goes to the
  product page elsewhere) on the right; a hairline under it once scrolled. Phones: logo, cart and «Comprar»; the menu in the drawer.
- **Type** — display headlines `clamp(36px, 5.2vw, 72px)`, line height 1.02, sentence case; letter-spaced caps only on eyebrows;
  body 16–17 px, line length ≤ 62ch. One display and one text font (the store's).
- **Spacing** — section gaps `clamp(56px, 8vw, 120px)` on an 8 px grid; one content width (1200 px) for every section.
- **Photos** — fixed ratios by role (hero 4:5, colour cards 4:5, detail 4:3, closing 21:9), radius 22 px everywhere, always
  `object-fit: cover`.
- **Colour** — paper, ink, one accent; bands use a light accent tint, never a dark text-only band; colourful mood = stronger tints
  + coloured letters (as built).
- **Buttons** — one pill shape; primary solid (ink or accent), secondary outline; height 48 px (laptop) / 52 px (phone).
- **Details** — hairline dividers, quiet card borders, visible focus rings, no grey-on-grey text.

## 3. Other templates' full-photo hero
`cover-full` look `right` (Crocante, Oh Belly and any template using it): the words move into a solid card in the store's paper
colour on the calmer side of the photo, all left-aligned, with padding and the radius above; on phones the card sits under the
photo. Only that look changes.

## 4. How YAPI fills it
The section library fill that already runs after a new template store is built fills Único's new layout (fixed mode). New or changed
catalogue entries: `hero-split` (title, text, eyebrow, cta, cta2, photo; pills = items ≤ 3, same rules as product-hero's pills),
`variant-cards` (title only; the cards come from the catalogue), `faq-split` look `lines`. Every existing rule holds: facts only,
numbers backed, a part without content is dropped, photos shown once before any repeats. The hero takes the most lifestyle-like
photo; the buy box keeps the product photos.

## 5. Edge cases
- No colours (or colours without their own photo): no colour cards, no «Ver colores» button, the hero's second button goes.
- One photo only: the hero shows it; the buy box shows it too (the product's own gallery is never empty); hotspots drop.
- Price still to confirm: «desde Bs …» hidden on cards; the buy box keeps today's behaviour.
- No reviews: the chip band uses product facts; no facts → it drops.
- Stores already built keep their files; only new builds and template switches to Único get the new page. Same for §3.

## 6. Testing
- Sandbox renders (as source-unico-lively.spec.ts): at 1280 and 390 the hero's words never overlap its photo; one text alignment
  in the hero panel; «Ver colores» shows only with colour cards; section gaps equal within ±8 px; no sideways scroll.
- Kit browser test: colour cards from variants with photos; tapping one selects that colour in the buy box and scrolls to it; hidden
  «desde» while the price is to confirm; none with < 2 photographed colours.
- `cover-full` `right`: the text card never overlaps the photo's centre third; left-aligned; readable contrast (≥ 4.5:1).
- Template contract suite for every template (the `cover-full` change touches several).
- Live: the sofa store with the owner's real photos, screenshots at 1280 and 390 next to nubecorp and Expanse.

## Out of scope
Growing stores (Único → catalogue), the browser button, the AliExpress API (each its own spec); a product video in the benefits band;
changing other templates beyond the `cover-full` `right` hero.
