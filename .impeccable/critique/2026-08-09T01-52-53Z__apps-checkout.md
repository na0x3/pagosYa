## Overall Impression

The storefront is genuinely well-crafted and defensively engineered (cart persistence, stock clamping, universal `escapeHtml()`), and the success screen is a real peak-end win. But the payment form itself — the highest-stakes screen in the whole app — has real gaps: no native `<form>` (Enter doesn't submit), field-level validation errors that vanish on tab switch, and a merchant-customizable accent color that can silently erase the primary CTA's entire visual identity. The single biggest opportunity: harden the payment form to match the polish already evident in the storefront and success screen.

## What's Working

- **Defensive cart/localStorage engineering**: stale-stock clamping, archived-item pruning, corrupt-JSON fallback, and universal `escapeHtml()` on all merchant-supplied content — a stress-tester genuinely cannot break this into a crash or XSS.
- **The success/receipt screen**: itemized lines, order ID, subtotal, and a same-screen WhatsApp/email recovery path — correctly exploits the peak-end effect.
- **Contact-field persistence across payment-method tab switches** — explicitly engineered so re-rendering never loses what the customer typed.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Merchant `accentColor` can silently collapse the storefront's primary CTA and its glow to invisible.**
Why it matters: live-verified on the actual seeded store (`accentColor: #000000`) — `body.has-custom-accent` aliases `--pg-accent-2` to `--pg-accent`, so the indigo→violet gradient CTA and its glow both go flat black-on-black with zero fallback. DESIGN.md calls this gradient checkout's deliberate fallback identity moment; any merchant with a dark accent silently breaks it.
Fix: validate/clamp merchant accent (reject too-dark/desaturated values) or derive `--pg-accent-2` via a guaranteed hue-shift instead of aliasing.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden apps/checkout` (accentColor validation)

**[P1] No `<form>` wraps the payment fields — Enter and mobile "Go" don't submit.**
Why it matters: universally expected checkout behavior; its absence is a real usability gap for a fast, thumb-driven customer, source-confirmed in `renderForm()`.
Fix: wrap the payment fields in a native `<form>` with a submit handler.
Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt apps/checkout` or a direct patch

**[P1] Validation errors aren't tied to the offending field and vanish on tab switch.**
Why it matters: live-verified — one generic red line near the Pay button, no field border/aria-invalid, and switching payment-method tabs clears the message even though the field is still invalid. Contradicts DESIGN.md's own Input/Error spec.
Fix: render each error under its own field (border + aria-invalid/aria-describedby); persist across tab switches until fixed.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify apps/checkout`

**[P1] Failed/canceled payment screens are dead ends, and the cart is already gone.**
Why it matters: source-confirmed — cart is cleared the moment "Ir a pagar" is clicked, so there's nothing to return to even on cancel; no retry action, no support contact, at the moment anxiety is highest.
Fix: add retry/return-to-store action and the same contact block used on the success receipt; don't clear the cart until payment actually succeeds.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden apps/checkout`

**[P2] "Token de prueba (modo test)" renders unconditionally to real customers.**
Why it matters: source-confirmed — no `import.meta.env.DEV` guard, unlike the correctly-gated "[dev] Simular confirmación exitosa" button elsewhere in the same file. A real paying customer would see "Visa (rechazada)" as a selectable option right above the pay button.
Fix: gate the test-token field behind the same dev-mode flag.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden apps/checkout`

**[P2] Storefront search input has no accessible label.**
Why it matters: source-confirmed — `#store-search` has neither `<label for>` nor `aria-label`, while the adjacent sort `<select>` correctly has one. Direct violation of DESIGN.md's own "placeholders are never a substitute for a label" rule.
Fix: add a matching `aria-label`.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify apps/checkout`

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (mobile, thumb-only)**: `.qty-stepper` buttons are a fixed 30×30px, under common touch-target minimums, with zero `@media` adjustment. No autosave — a backgrounded/reloaded tab drops name/email/phone with no warning.

**Riley (stress tester)**: refreshing mid-payment silently loses everything (`client_secret` never lands in the URL) — no "session expired" message, just a bare reload to the empty store link. `PHONE_PATTERN` accepts any 6+ digit string ("12345678" passes as valid).

**Jordan (first-timer, unfamiliar with local rails)**: the four payment tabs (Tarjeta/Tigo Money/Transferencia/QR) are icon+label only, zero explanatory copy on what each requires. The QR requires-action screen names no app, gives no fallback, no visible expiry countdown.
