# Café Aroma independent storefront

Scope: this standalone demonstration, not the pagosYa platform identity or all future generated websites.

The user requested individually designed professional business pages and customer-owned source code, then authorized the next implementation steps. The agent selected café/restaurant as the first example and option A (`../../.impeccable/mocks/independent-cafe/option-a.png`) as a composition reference. This was an agent selection under that authorization, **not explicit user approval of a comp**. Concept seed: `004026f8`.

## THESIS

A warm café page should make choosing a coffee and adding it to an order feel as direct as reading a café menu.

## OWN-WORLD

Ivory paper, espresso text, terracotta accents, high-contrast Instrument Serif and quiet DM Sans. A newly produced breakfast photograph supplies the material and atmosphere. No invented testimonials, opening hours, sourcing claims, or commercial evidence. Instrument Serif is an intentional readable implementation choice; the generated reference's condensed glyphs are not a font specification.

## STORY

Name and appetite first; then the complete real demonstration catalog, category choices, and order summary. A short café passage supplies the “Nuestra mesa” destination. Demonstration labels explain that these orders do not generate charges.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

A restrained masthead, two-line editorial headline, one direct menu action, and a large breakfast photograph. At desktop widths the text and photograph share a split hero. On mobile, text precedes the photograph. No caption badge over the photograph.

## FORM

Seed `004026f8`. Flat, square surfaces and thin warm dividers. Menu rows replace product cards; circular add controls remain visible beside prices. The order panel follows the menu on mobile. Instrument Serif retains its natural letterforms and responsive size. SVG icons use a consistent thin stroke. Focus is explicit, controls remain usable by touch, and reduced-motion preferences disable smooth scrolling and transitions.

## Reference translation

Preserved: split hero, light paper, strong serif headline, real coffee/pastry photograph, dark rectangular CTA, terracotta details, category tabs, ruled menu list and order column. Adapted: verified catalog names/prices, demo labels, valid empty-order behavior, locally hosted fonts and accessible mobile stacking. These choices are implementation decisions within the user's broad direction and can be reviewed independently of the reusable generation engine.
