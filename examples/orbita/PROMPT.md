# ÓRBITA / Equipo para otros mundos

Create a complete, highly art-directed Spanish-language ecommerce store for ÓRBITA, a fictional premium outdoor label inspired by retro space exploration and expeditions to Mars. This is wearable outdoor fashion and everyday equipment, not actual space-rated protective equipment. All product catalog data and supplied photographs are approved demo content. The website must be designed and implemented by pagosYa's AI generator. Make the composition memorable and specific to this brand.

## Creative direction

Think of an expedition field manual transformed into a premium editorial store. Massive confident condensed grotesk typography, tiny monospaced equipment codes, thin technical rules, mission numbering, an orbital-circle insignia, warm bone #F0EEE6, ink #191B19 and saturated vermilion #EF451F. Real cinematic photography and generous whitespace. Square industrial controls with subtle clipped details. Use the campaign photograph at large scale, without covering the hiker. Hero headline: “EQUIPO PARA / OTROS MUNDOS.” Small kicker “ÓRBITA / EXPEDICIÓN 001”. CTA “Preparar mi expedición”. A quiet secondary link “Explorar la misión”. The first fold should feel like a magazine cover, with clear readable typography and visible equipment photography. A huge typographic ORBITA moment near the footer would reinforce the identity.

Give the page a strong rhythm: compact technical navigation; cinematic campaign hero; narrow expedition information strip; beautifully photographed shop collection; striking vermilion editorial mission section with huge typography and an orbit/trajectory motif; practical buying FAQ; impressive typographic footer. Every section must feel like the same distinct brand. Avoid generic rounded cards, ubiquitous pill badges, purple gradients, emoji, fake reviews, fake certifications and meaningless metrics. Fictional mission labels are visual storytelling, not scientific claims. Include a subtle “Colección conceptual · Tienda de demostración” footer note.

## Real shopping experience

Use the four EXISTING catalog products with all their saved variants, prices, inventory and extras. Do not recreate or duplicate them. Each product card opens its own product detail page. Add a visible cart count and usable cart/review route. Product detail layout: big product photo, small equipment code, product name, price that reacts to choices, short description, separate clearly labeled groups of options, optional additions with their prices, quantity, and a strong add-to-cart button. Group selectors by Color, Talla, Corte, Capacidad, Kit, Tapa, Misión, Presentación, Estuche as appropriate. Never flatten all combinations into one huge dropdown. Make this clear once in product details as “Fotografía del color de portada”. Parka = bone-white jacket photo; Mochila = vermilion backpack; Botella = graphite flask; Kit = pouch and patches flatlay. Never confuse the campaign photo with a product photo.

Design the product and checkout pages with the same orbital identity, typography and industrial precision as the homepage. Reuse the supported pagosYa commerce runtime hooks for stock, variants, extras and checkout; ensure authored CSS also styles the commerce elements. Keep any native runtime controls functional. Motion should be restrained: a slow orbital line or small entrance transitions, respecting reduced motion.
