# Premium Savia implementation

The selected design is candidate A, developed by Codex after two visual alternatives and independent real Claude Code critique. Shared components use semantic store variables, with confirmed brand references for new designs and explicit authored values taking precedence during local edits. The version 3 manifest records authored, statically resolved or unresolved values; it does not claim to reproduce the browser's full cascade. Historical version 2 manifests remain readable.

Savia's photography now includes eight coordinated studio/ritual assets. Bottle color changes show the matching photographed bottle; juice presentation changes show an individual bottle or pack; granola flavor changes show Original or Cacao. Size, price, stock limits, quantity, subtotal and cart lines use actual catalog data. Product descriptions are shown once, and detail tags are factual. Homepage, product, cart and checkout share surfaces, typography and controls. The video is bundled and has native controls.

Reusable changes include planner-selected direction/rationale, a nine-variable style contract, portable variant assets, current-runtime color inheritance, ambiguous partial-photo protection, correct available starting prices, actual option-count summaries, product-aware visual capture and review metadata, and MP4 preservation in public assets. Supported variant products mount their authored design on the customer route. Unsupported extras and malformed matrices retain the established configurator. Unit tests cover the sandbox route and stable variant payloads.

Browser evidence: final Savia at 1440 desktop and 390 mobile; additional product checks at 320, 768 and 1280 have no horizontal overflow and all inspected option/quantity controls meet the 44px target. Visible images and fonts loaded. The video reported readyState 4 and duration 28.9 seconds; playback was also checked during the initial demonstration.

A final mixed cart contained two Coral / 750 ml bottles (Bs 218), Cacao / 500 g granola (Bs 65), and 750 ml / Pack de 3 juice (Bs 96). The exact names, quantities and Bs 379 total persisted through successful simulated checkout. No charge or real order was created. The local customer route at port 5175 was also inspected and showed the authored product page inside its sandbox; hosted checkout payloads were checked by automated tests. The standalone full checkout was exercised in Chrome; do not infer that a real payment processor was charged.

Claude was invoked through the authenticated CLI with read-only tools, no interactive permission prompts, Opus, and per-review budgets. Prompts, outputs and raw receipts are retained for implementation critique, A/B candidate critique and final code review. Codex made and verified the edits. Four final-code-review findings were fixed: store/brand precedence, dark overlay tokens, invalid product capture reported as 400, and exact brand-variable matching. Payment failures now also appear beside the checkout action while leaving the chosen variant intact and allowing retry. The optional review-form identity/race finding is pre-existing and remains outside this demo, which does not expose review forms.

Visual acceptance is provisional against the user's written feedback and reference because the user was away. Candidate B is archived in this review folder; A is the delivered storefront. No automated beauty score or Amboras-equivalence claim is made. The six-store generation pilot and its preserved failures are documented in ../pilot/README.md. Durable production review orchestration and the larger paired benchmark remain conditional follow-up work.
