# Star Wars comic-store demonstration

The user wants a working Star Wars store like the Thunder & Pulp example in their Amboras screenshot. This is an isolated local demo in pagosYa, not a change to an existing merchant's live store.

Visitors inspect a comic cover, switch between three actual published comics, choose a single issue or a sample collection, explore and search the catalog, review a product, and complete a no-charge purchase simulation.

The source of truth for art direction is the supplied screenshot: yellow, red, cream and black; compact comic-shop branding; a large warm bookstore image to the left of a yellow product heading and black buying panel. Spanish UI and sample BOB prices fit the existing workspace. All inventory/prices and the three-comic bundle are demonstration data. Published artwork has source credits. Generated imagery depicts a conceptual shop backdrop and makes no claim about a real shop.
